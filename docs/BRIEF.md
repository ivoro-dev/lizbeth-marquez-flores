# Client brief (summary)

Full original: `client-assets/00-brief/Portfolio_Build_Brief_for_Developer.docx` (don't read it; this summary and `src/content/` contain everything).

**Client:** Lizbeth Marquez-Flores, The Brand Formula. Brand strategist, 17 yrs clinical healthcare, 7 yrs founding a patented haircare brand, certified in Brand Management (London Business School), bilingual EN/ES.

**Purpose:** job applications, not client acquisition. Readers: HR teams at large companies and owners of small ones. Must show **thinking** (what was wrong, what I decided, what I made, what happened). Visuals are evidence, not the point.

**Main target role:** Manager, Brand Storyteller at dsm-firmenich (consumer health brands Culturelle, AZO, Estroven). They want brand storytelling, message architecture, verbal identity, editorial content, interviewing, earned media, consumer PR. So the interview/press links must be easy to find: they want to see and hear her.

**Tone:** confident and clear, with personality. Not corporate-stiff, not casual. Fun typography and light animation welcome; content stays substantive.

**Order:** Cover → About → 01 Locks of Liz → 02 Wholeness Acupuncture Center → 03 Boss Babes & Brunch → 04 Work Drama → Listen & Watch → Approach (The Diagnosis) → Contact.

**Must-haves**

- Every project follows challenge → decision → work → result. Decision is the most important.
- No invented or implied results. Where there's a number, use it; otherwise say in progress.
- All four Listen & Watch links clickable.
- Full name legible on the cover (old site's photo cut it into "FLO RES").
- Wholeness before/after labelled "Profile positioning and bio rewrite". (Updated by client: also show the one-month-later follower growth, via screenshots only.)

**Design from client:** colours teal #629FA9, orange #FF7828, black, white. Max 2–3 fonts used consistently. Body ≥ 14pt (we use ≥ 16px). Light animation on the landing page.

**Deliverables:** PDF (her priority) + landing page with same content. This build = landing page first; PDF later.
